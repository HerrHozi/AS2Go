PHASE 06

Reconnaissance (Compromised)

With a compromised account, an attacker can investigate the directory and identify useful accounts, computers, groups, and accessible resources before attempting to gain additional privileges.

What this phase shows

Surveys forest and domain information, privileged group memberships, computer accounts, and domain controllers, then attempts to list configured administrative shares. Optional extended tasks include DNS, session, and honeytoken activity.

Security lesson

A standard domain account can reveal valuable directory information. Detect unusual enumeration and honeytoken activity, and review permissions on administrative resources.
