PHASE 07

Memory Access (Memory Dump)

This phase demonstrates how an attacker with local administrator or SYSTEM rights reads LSASS process memory on a compromised host, extracts hashes, tickets, and credential keys, and reuses them for further attacks in the authorized lab.

What this phase shows

Covers gaining elevated access, opening the secured lsass.exe process in memory, dumping it with tools such as Mimikatz, ProcDump, or comsvcs.dll, extracting NTLM hashes, Kerberos tickets, and credential keys, and reusing them through Pass-the-Hash, Pass-the-Ticket, or Kerberoasting.

Security lesson

Protect LSASS with Credential Guard and process protection, restrict local administrator membership, and alert on handle access to LSASS and on known memory dump tooling.
