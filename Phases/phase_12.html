PHASE 12

Domain Compromise & Persistence

The final scenario stage demonstrates persistence and impact after privileged access has been obtained, including changes to domain accounts, policies, and authentication material.

What this phase shows

Offers backdoor accounts, Group Policy Template changes, user and privileged-group manipulation, staged file encryption, domain DPAPI backup-key export, Golden Ticket creation and injection, and configured domain-controller restart workflows.

Security lesson

A domain compromise can leave multiple persistence paths. Monitor privileged account and policy changes, protect domain-controller secrets, and include forged tickets and exposed backup keys in recovery planning.
